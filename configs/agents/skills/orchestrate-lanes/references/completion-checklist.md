# Completion aid

Use in conversation/context when useful, not as a persisted checklist or report
schema. Start with the user's original and refined outcomes and explicit scope
changes, not the last assignments or a green test suite.

## Inspect before calling it complete

- Does the actual target deliver each requested outcome? Inspect current source,
  dependent wiring, and the representative product path. Source-ready reports,
  compilation, mocks, or screenshots alone do not prove product acceptance.
- Does the evidence distinguish the intended behavior from relevant negative
  controls, such as typing versus paste, multiple choices, or normal startup?
  Keep measured results separate from source concerns and unverified claims.
  Fake-provider checks do not prove LLM decisions or skill quality.
- Were concrete findings verified against the current coherent source? Are
  required formatting, lint, tests, architecture/plugin checks, and product
  acceptance covered as applicable? State exact commands/results and unavailable
  checks; relevant later edits may require revalidation.
- Has a fresh supported observation of every worker's own history and runtime
  work accounted for active writers, commands, interactions, queued work,
  uncertain admissions, and ownership transfers? Follow bounded cursors and
  disclose incomplete coverage. Requested steering mode, cancellation requests,
  idle sessions, and worker reports alone do not establish settlement.
- Is useful authorized work still actionable? Continue it. Review the acceptance
  scenario before adding workers; do not create generic polish work or silently
  narrow the goal to match what was delivered.

## Honest stopping

Successful completion means requested outcomes are supported by evidence and
relevant work has settled, not merely that workers finished their assignments.
Summarize outcomes, actual validation, and limitations in the session.

When an external dependency blocks remaining work, first finish other safe work,
then name the unmet outcome, exact blocker, and next action or authority needed.
When stopped, interrupted, or failed, preserve work and report known live-work
and ownership disposition plus reconciliation steps. Do not claim success,
transfer ownership prematurely, retry ambiguous effects, or promise automatic
restart. No separate orchestration document is needed.
